Email walidabahri@gmail.com. There is no account to sign in to, and the developer cannot see or recover the vault on your phone. Please never email photos of your documents.

What does Fotosello scan for?

Photos of ID cards, passports, DNI/NIE and driver's licences, bank and credit cards, screenshots that show passwords, codes or bank details, and other documents such as contracts, letters and receipts. It uses on-device text recognition, document detection and rules. It does not look for nudity or intimate photos. Detection is not perfect, so review the results before you move or remove anything.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. The scan, the results and the encrypted vault all stay on your device. Fotosello has no servers and works in Airplane Mode. It also has no analytics or ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or email. That also means the developer cannot see, recover or reset your vault.

Is there a cloud backup?

No, not in this version. Your vault lives only on this phone. To keep a copy, open a photo in the vault and tap Save to Photos or Share (copies you save or share leave the vault and are not encrypted). Viewing and exporting are always free. Do this before you delete the app or change phones.

What is free?

Scanning, reviewing, and up to 10 photos in your vault. Fotosello Pro removes the limit. It is offered as a weekly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Fotosello Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with. Restoring brings back Pro; it does not bring back vault photos, because those are never stored anywhere else.

How do I cancel a subscription on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Fotosello and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I cancel a subscription on Android?

Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Choose Fotosello and tap Cancel subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Fotosello stores, including the vault, lives only on your phone, so removing the app removes it. On iPhone, touch and hold the icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. On Android, touch and hold the icon and tap Uninstall. There is no server copy, so export anything you want to keep first.

Did Fotosello delete my original photos?

Only if you chose to remove the originals after moving them. iPhone and many Android phones keep removed photos in Recently Deleted (or Trash) for up to 30 days. Empty it to remove them completely.

Ayuda en español

Escribe a walidabahri@gmail.com. Nunca envíes fotos de tus documentos por correo.

¿Qué busca Fotosello?

Fotos del DNI, NIE, pasaporte y carné de conducir, tarjetas bancarias, capturas con contraseñas, códigos o datos bancarios, y otros documentos como contratos, cartas y recibos. Usa reconocimiento de texto en el dispositivo, detección de documentos y reglas. No busca desnudos ni fotos íntimas. La detección no es perfecta: revisa los resultados antes de mover o eliminar nada.

¿Mis fotos salen del móvil?

No. El análisis, los resultados y la bóveda cifrada se quedan en tu dispositivo. Fotosello no tiene servidores, funciona en modo avión y no tiene analítica ni anuncios.

¿Necesito una cuenta?

No. No hay registro, inicio de sesión ni correo. Por eso nadie, ni el desarrollador, puede ver, recuperar ni restablecer tu bóveda.

¿Hay copia en la nube?

No en esta versión. Tu bóveda solo está en este móvil. Para guardar una copia, abre una foto de la bóveda y toca Guardar en Fotos o Compartir (las copias salen de la bóveda y no están cifradas). Ver y exportar es siempre gratis. Hazlo antes de borrar la app o cambiar de móvil.

¿Cómo restauro mis compras?

Abre la pantalla de Fotosello Pro y toca Restaurar compras, con el mismo Apple Account o la misma cuenta de Google con la que compraste. Se recupera Pro, no las fotos de la bóveda.

¿Cómo cancelo la suscripción?

iPhone: Ajustes, toca tu nombre, Suscripciones, Fotosello, Cancelar suscripción (al menos 24 horas antes de la renovación). Android: app Google Play Store, foto de perfil, Pagos y suscripciones, Suscripciones, Fotosello, Cancelar suscripción. Borrar la app no cancela la suscripción.

¿Cómo borro mis datos?

Borra la app: todo lo que guarda Fotosello, incluida la bóveda, está solo en tu móvil. En iPhone, mantén pulsado el icono, Eliminar app y Eliminar app. En Android, mantén pulsado el icono y toca Desinstalar. No hay copia en ningún servidor, así que exporta antes lo que quieras conservar.

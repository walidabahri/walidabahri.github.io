Privacy Support

Fotosello does not collect data. The scan and the vault stay on your phone.

Find the ID, card and password photos in your camera roll, and seal them.

Fotosello scans your photos on your device for IDs, passports, bank cards, password screenshots and other documents, then locks the ones you choose in an AES-256 encrypted vault. No account, no server, no analytics.

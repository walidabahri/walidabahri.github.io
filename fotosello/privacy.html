Privacy policy

Effective date: to be set on publication. This policy covers the Fotosello mobile app for iPhone and Android (bundle / package ID com.walidabahri.fotosello).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Walid Abahri, an individual developer, Calle Pablo Neruda 3, 6E, 01009 Vitoria-Gasteiz, Álava, Spain ("we", "us"). Contact: walidabahri@gmail.com.

2. The short version

Fotosello is built so that your photos never leave your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers that receive your photos or your vault. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Photo library access

Fotosello asks for access to your photo library so that it can:

  • scan your photos on your device to find ones that may be sensitive, such as ID documents (ID cards, passports, DNI/NIE), bank cards, screenshots containing passwords or bank details, and other documents. The scan uses on-device text recognition (Apple Vision on iPhone), document detection and simple rules. It does not analyse photos for nudity or intimate content;
  • show you the results so you can choose what to protect;
  • copy the photos you choose into the encrypted vault and, only if you ask, delete the originals from your library.

All of this happens on your device. Your photos, scan results and vault contents are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can limit or withdraw photo access at any time in your device settings. If you do, you can still pick photos manually.

4. The vault

Items you move into the vault are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The vault is protected by Face ID / biometrics or a PIN that stays on your device. We cannot see, recover or reset your vault. There is no cloud backup: deleting the app or losing your device may make your vault contents unrecoverable unless you exported them first.

5. No analytics or tracking

Fotosello does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not send usage data, device identifiers or crash data to us. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may pass anonymised crash reports to us under their own terms.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. The app only receives confirmation of whether you have an active purchase, which it checks on your device. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

7. Support emails

If you email walidabahri@gmail.com, we receive only what you choose to send: your email address, your message and any attachments. We use it only to reply to you. Support email is handled through Google's Gmail service, so your message is stored in our Gmail account. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. Please do not email photos of documents or other sensitive content; we never need them to help you. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes. The only third parties involved are Apple or Google for purchases, and Google as our email provider for the support emails you choose to send.

9. International transfers

We do not transfer your photos or any app data anywhere. Gmail is provided by Google (for users in the European Economic Area, by Google Ireland Limited). Google may process email on servers outside the EEA, including in the United States, and says it relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

10. Children

Fotosello is not directed to children under 14 (the age of digital consent in Spain under the LOPDGDD). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests can be met by deleting the app. For anything else, email walidabahri@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es).

12. Security

Encryption keys are stored in the device's secure storage (Keychain / Secure Enclave on iPhone, Android Keystore on Android). No security is perfect. Protect your device passcode and your vault PIN.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, the updated text will be published on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will also be shown in the app.

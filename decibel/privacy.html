Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026. This policy covers the Decibel Meter app for iPhone (bundle ID com.walidabahri.decibel).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Walid Abahri, an individual developer, Calle Pablo Neruda 3, 6E, 01009 Vitoria-Gasteiz, Álava, Spain ("we", "us"). Contact: walidabahri@gmail.com.

2. The short version

Decibel Meter is built so that nothing leaves your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers. It does not record audio. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Microphone

Decibel Meter asks for access to the microphone so that it can measure sound levels. When the meter or a recording you started is running:

  • the app reads short blocks of microphone audio in memory on your device, calculates the sound level (for example dB(A), Leq, minimum, maximum and peak), and immediately discards the audio;
  • no audio is recorded, saved to a file or sent anywhere, and the app does not recognise speech or identify sounds;
  • the microphone stops as soon as you stop the meter or the recording. A background recording (Decibel Pro) runs only after you start it, and iOS shows the microphone indicator while it runs.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in your iPhone settings. The meter cannot work without it.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores, only on your iPhone: your saved sessions (date, time, duration, one sound-level value per second, statistics and events over your alert threshold), the notes you add, your calibration offset and your settings. This data is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to us. Reports (PDF or CSV) are created on your device and leave it only if you choose to share or save them, using the iOS share sheet; the app you share them with then handles them under its own terms. You can delete a session in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app.

5. Alerts, widgets and Live Activity

Level alerts use local notifications created on your device; no push service is used. The Home Screen widgets and the Live Activity show data from your device only.

6. No analytics or tracking

Decibel Meter does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not send usage data, device identifiers or crash data to us. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass anonymised crash reports to us under its own terms.

7. Purchases

The Decibel Pro subscription and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. The app only receives confirmation of whether you have an active purchase, which it checks on your device. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

8. Support emails

Support email is handled through Google's Gmail service, so your message is stored in our Gmail account. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes. The only third parties involved are Apple for purchases, and Google as our email provider for the support emails you choose to send.

11. Children

Decibel Meter is not directed to children under 14 (the age of digital consent in Spain under the LOPDGDD). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests can be met by deleting the app. For anything else, email walidabahri@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es).

13. Security

Your sessions are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection when your device is locked. No security is perfect. Protect your device with a passcode.

Add an entry

Tap the plus button. Choose “I lent” or “I borrowed”, enter an amount, pick a person (or add one), and save. Amounts are stored in cents so partial payments stay exact.

Payments

Open a person, then mark an entry paid or add a partial payment. A partial payment cannot be larger than what is left. The home cards “Owed to me” and “I owe” add up open remainders. Each person’s balance is the net of what they still owe you minus what you still owe them.

Reminders

Turn on “Remind me on due date” when you save an entry. The phone asks for notification permission the first time. The reminder is scheduled on the device for 9:00 on the due date. If that time has already passed, the entry is still saved and no reminder is set. Android may deliver it inexactly; Owed does not use the exact-alarm permission.

Export and delete

Settings → Export CSV shares a spreadsheet of your ledger. Settings → Delete all data erases people and entries on this device. On a person’s page, “Delete person” removes that person and their entries after you confirm.

Demo data

Settings → Demo data for screenshots adds sample people. Turn it off to remove the samples. Entries you added yourself are kept.

Languages, currency, appearance

Settings includes EUR (the default), USD, GBP, MAD, and AED, plus English, Spanish, and Arabic. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Appearance follows the system, or you can force light or dark.

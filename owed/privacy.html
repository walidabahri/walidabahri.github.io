Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026. This policy covers the Owed mobile app (bundle / package id com.walidabahri.owed).

Contact: walidabahri@gmail.com

What Owed stores

If you choose to, you can save people, optional phone numbers, notes, amounts, dates, and payment history. Those records are written only to storage on your device (Capacitor Preferences, which uses the platform’s on-device preferences storage). Owed does not send them to the developer or to any other server.

What Owed does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or cloud backup.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.
  • The app does not move money and does not connect to a bank.

Reminders, export, and sharing

Due-date reminders are local notifications scheduled by the operating system on your phone. They are not pushed from a server. On Android they may arrive around 9:00 on the due date rather than at that exact minute, because the app does not use exact-alarm permissions.

CSV export and “Send reminder” open your device’s share sheet, or on the web a download / clipboard copy. The text or file goes only to the app you pick. Owed does not receive a copy. The destination app has its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

Settings → Delete all data removes people and entries from the device. You can also delete one person (and that person’s entries) or a single entry. Uninstalling Owed removes its on-device storage. There is no server copy to delete, and there is no way for the developer to restore a lost phone.

Children

Owed is a general personal ledger. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page with a new effective date. The app does not download the policy.

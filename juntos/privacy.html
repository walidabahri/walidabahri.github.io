Privacy policy · Juntos: Conversation Cards

Effective date: 8 November 2026. This policy covers the Juntos: Conversation Cards app ("Juntos") for iPhone (bundle ID com.walidabahri.juntos).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Walid Abahri, an individual developer, Calle Pablo Neruda 3, 6E, 01009 Vitoria-Gasteiz, Álava, Spain ("we", "us"). Contact: walidabahri@gmail.com.

2. The short version

Juntos is built so that nothing leaves your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers, and it works without an internet connection. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. The cards

All conversation cards are included in the app. Choosing a mode, a level or a card, playing pass the phone, and your answers (which you say out loud) are never recorded or sent anywhere. The names you type for pass the phone are kept only on your iPhone, for that game.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores, only on your iPhone: your favourite cards, the names you entered for pass the phone, the daily card, and your settings (such as language, modes and levels). This data is not sent to us. You can remove favourites in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app.

5. Widget and sharing

The Home Screen widget shows the daily question, which the app shares with the widget on your iPhone through an App Group. When you share a card, the image is created on your device and leaves it only if you choose to share or save it with the iOS share sheet; the app you share it with then handles it under its own terms. Juntos has no in-app option to save to your photo library and never asks for access to your photos.

6. No analytics or tracking

Juntos does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not send usage data, device identifiers or crash data to us. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass anonymised crash reports to us under its own terms.

7. Purchases

The Juntos Pro yearly subscription and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store, including Family Sharing. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. The app only receives confirmation of whether you have an active purchase, which it checks on your device. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

8. Support emails

If you email walidabahri@gmail.com, we receive only what you choose to send: your email address, your message and any attachments. We use it only to reply to you. Support email is handled through Google's Gmail service, so your message is stored in our Gmail account. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes. The only third parties involved are Apple for purchases, and Google as our email provider for the support emails you choose to send.

10. International transfers

We do not transfer any app data anywhere. Gmail is provided by Google (for users in the European Economic Area, by Google Ireland Limited). Google may process email on servers outside the EEA, including in the United States, and says it relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

11. Children and families

Juntos includes cards that families can play together with children, but the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It is not a Kids category app, and purchases are made by the account holder through Apple, which offers parental controls such as Ask to Buy. Support email is meant for adults; if you are under 14 (the age of digital consent in Spain under the LOPDGDD), please ask a parent or guardian to write to us.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold no app data about you, most requests can be met by deleting the app. For anything else, email walidabahri@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es).

13. Security

Your favourites and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection when your device is locked. No security is perfect. Protect your device with a passcode.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, the updated text will be published on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will also be shown in the app.

15. Contact
